CyberPulse: Shadow Net is a text-driven cyber RPG set inside a black-market data economy. You start as an unranked operative with a burner rig, a thin stack of credits, and one hundred units of energy. Everything after that is your call.

Scan the net for live targets, deploy keyloggers onto careless smartphones, inject payloads into corporate mainframes, and intercept transfers mid-route. Each run burns energy and risks your stealth rating — get caught and the trace costs you. Move earnings into a cold offline vault before you touch the dice tables, because anything sitting in your hot wallet is fair game when a wager goes bad.

01 — The economy

A closed loop of credits, energy and trust.

Nothing here touches real currency. Every credit is virtual, minted by the simulation and destroyed by your losses. What makes it interesting is scarcity: energy regenerates slowly, stealth degrades under pressure, and the vault is the only place funds are truly safe.

  • Energy caps at 100 and ticks back on its own. Stims buy you speed at a price.
  • Stealth rises with clean runs and drops when a trace lands on you.
  • Vault funds cannot be wagered, seized, or lost — only what's hot is at risk.

03 — Speculative wagering

The dice table under the server farm.

A provably simple high/low engine sits at the bottom of the operations deck. Call the roll above or below the median for a modest multiplier, or name the exact face for a payout that will fund a week of downtime. Multipliers shift with the risk you accept, and the house never fronts you credit — you can only wager what's hot in your wallet.
